401  /admin
409  /conflict
503  /slow

a 3xx names where to go instead:
HTTP/1.1 302 Found
Location: /index.html

401 asks who you are; 403 has already decided:
  no cookie          401
  cookie role=guest  403

a 5xx says the server failed, not that you asked wrongly:
HTTP/1.1 503 Service Unavailable
